# Apparel & Textiles Data Analytics Internship Project

## 📊 Project Overview

This repository contains my **6-week Apparel & Textiles Data Analytics Internship Project**, focused on applying data analytics techniques to a fictional apparel and fashion retail company, **StyleWear Pvt. Ltd.**

The project covers the complete data analytics lifecycle, including:

- Data Collection
- Data Cleaning
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Data Integration and Transformation
- KPI Development
- Power BI Dashboard Development
- Trend Analysis
- Forecasting Methodology
- Business Insights
- Strategic Recommendations

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how data analytics can support business decisions related to **product portfolios, pricing, customer segments, inventory planning, fashion trends, and future demand planning**.

---

# 🏢 Business Scenario

### Company: StyleWear Pvt. Ltd.

**Industry:** Apparel & Fashion Retail

**Business Model:** Online and Offline Fashion Retail

StyleWear Pvt. Ltd. is a fictional fashion retail company that sells apparel and fashion products through online and offline channels.

The company wants to use data analytics to better understand its product portfolio, pricing structure, product categories, customer segments, and fashion-related patterns.

---

# 🎯 Business Problems

The company is facing several business challenges:

1. Difficulty identifying high-performing brands
2. Limited visibility into product pricing
3. Difficulty understanding customer segments
4. Inefficient inventory planning
5. Changing customer fashion preferences
6. Increasing competition in the apparel market
7. Difficulty planning future demand

Data analytics can help the company convert available data into meaningful business insights and support better decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Collect relevant apparel and fashion datasets
- Understand the structure of raw datasets
- Clean and prepare the data
- Handle missing values and duplicate records
- Standardize data formats and categories
- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Analyze brands, gender, pricing, and colors
- Transform and integrate available datasets
- Create meaningful KPIs
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Analyze product and pricing trends
- Understand forecasting approaches
- Identify business opportunities and risks
- Provide data-driven strategic recommendations
