# Week 4 — Power BI Dashboard Development

## Overview

Week 4 focused on developing an interactive Power BI dashboard for the apparel and textiles business scenario.

The dashboard converts the findings from the previous data analysis stages into an interactive visual format that can support business decision-making.

## Objectives

- Create an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Develop meaningful business KPIs
- Analyze product and brand information
- Analyze product pricing
- Understand gender distribution
- Provide interactive filtering
- Present business insights visually

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

- Total Products
- Total Brands
- Average Product Price
- Highest Product Price
- Lowest Product Price
- Total Colors

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the apparel product portfolio and pricing.

## Visualizations

The dashboard includes visualizations such as:

- Product Count by Brand
- Product Distribution by Gender
- Average Price by Brand

These charts help stakeholders understand product portfolios, customer segments, and pricing patterns.

## Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for:

- Product Brand
- Gender
- Primary Color

These filters allow users to interactively explore the data and examine specific product segments.

## Business Purpose

The dashboard provides stakeholders with a single interactive view of important apparel business metrics.

Users can move from high-level KPIs to detailed analysis by using the charts and slicers.

For example, management can select a particular brand, gender category, or primary color to investigate the corresponding products and pricing information.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- Data visualization
- Excel/CSV data

The Power BI dashboard transformed the analyzed apparel data into an interactive business reporting tool.

It provides a simple way to monitor product count, brands, pricing, gender distribution, and color information.
