# Week 5 — Trend Analysis & Forecasting

## Overview

Week 5 focused on identifying trends in the apparel and textiles dataset and understanding how forecasting techniques can support future business decisions.

The analysis combined quantitative observations from the available dataset with conceptual forecasting approaches.

## Objectives

- Identify important apparel industry trends
- Analyze pricing and product patterns
- Understand brand and customer-related trends
- Explain forecasting techniques
- Identify potential business opportunities and risks
- Develop recommendations for future forecasting

## Trend Analysis

The analysis focused on:

### Brand Trends
Different brands have different levels of product presence in the catalog.

### Pricing Trends
Products are available across different price ranges, indicating different customer segments.

### Customer Trends
Products are distributed across different gender categories, supporting customer segmentation.

### Product Trends
Certain colors and product attributes occur more frequently than others.

## Forecasting Methods

Three forecasting approaches were evaluated:

### Moving Average

Moving averages can smooth short-term fluctuations and help identify underlying trends.

### Linear Regression

Linear regression can be used to study relationships between variables such as price, demand, marketing spending, and sales.

### Time-Series Forecasting

Time-series methods such as ARIMA and Exponential Smoothing can be used to forecast future demand when historical time-based sales data is available.

## Important Limitation

The available apparel dataset does not contain historical monthly or yearly sales data.

Therefore, an actual numerical sales forecast was not performed.

Forecasting was evaluated conceptually. In a real-world implementation, historical sales data containing dates, units sold, revenue, discounts, and customer segments would be required.

## Business Impact

Trend and forecasting analysis can help an apparel business improve:

- Inventory planning
- Pricing decisions
- Product development
- Marketing strategies
- Demand planning
