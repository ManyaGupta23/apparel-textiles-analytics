# Week 6 — Final Strategic Business Report

## 📊 Overview

Week 6 represents the final stage of the Apparel & Textiles Data Analytics Project.

This stage brings together the work completed during Weeks 1–5:

- Data Collection & Cleaning
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Data Integration & Transformation
- Power BI Dashboard Development
- Trend Analysis
- Forecasting Methodology

The objective of Week 6 is to consolidate the analytical findings and translate them into meaningful business insights and strategic recommendations for the fictional company **StyleWear Pvt. Ltd.**

---

## 🏢 Business Scenario

**Company:** StyleWear Pvt. Ltd.

**Industry:** Apparel & Fashion Retail

**Business Model:** Online and Offline Fashion Retail

The company wants to use data analytics to better understand its product portfolio, pricing, brands, gender distribution, colors, trends, inventory planning, and future demand.

---

## 🎯 Final Project Objectives

The complete project aimed to:

- Collect apparel and fashion datasets
- Clean and prepare raw data
- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis
- Transform and standardize data
- Create category mappings
- Develop business KPIs
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Analyze product and pricing trends
- Evaluate forecasting methodologies
- Generate business insights
- Develop strategic recommendations
