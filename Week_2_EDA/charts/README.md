# Week 2 — EDA Charts

## Overview

This folder contains the visualizations created during Week 2 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of the apparel and fashion dataset.

The charts were created to identify important patterns in product brands, gender categories, pricing, and product colors.

## Charts Included

### 1. Brand-wise Product Distribution

Shows the number of products associated with different brands.

**Purpose:** To identify brands with larger product portfolios.

### 2. Product Distribution by Gender

Shows the distribution of products across available gender categories.

**Purpose:** To understand the product focus across different customer segments.

### 3. Product Price Distribution

Shows how product prices are distributed across the dataset.

**Purpose:** To understand the pricing range and different price segments.

### 4. Product Color Distribution

Shows the frequency of different primary colors.

**Purpose:** To identify commonly represented colors in the product catalog.

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab / Jupyter Notebook

## Purpose of Visualizations

These visualizations support the EDA report by converting numerical and categorical data into an easy-to-understand visual format. The findings were later used for dashboard development and business interpretation.
